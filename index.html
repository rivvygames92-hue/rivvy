How to play

Tap to flip between the floor and the ceiling.
Swipe up or down to switch rails.
Swipe forward to dash-slice enemies ahead.
Swipe back to slice the ones chasing you.

Escape the Factory Coming in the next update

A new way to play: 18 stages across 3 zones of the factory. Each stage is a short run that ends with a boss. Your progress is saved, earn up to 3 stars a stage, and beat each zone's giant boss to win its costume and break out.

Zone 1 · Assembly Line

Cream tiles and roller belts. Free to play.
Scrap King
A heap of scrap that rebuilds itself into a giant. Cut its glowing core when it kneels.
Wins: Scrap costume

Zone 2 · Foundry

Rusty iron, molten seams and a hot chain.
Rail Ripper
A huge excavator that tears holes in the rails. Cut the claw when it gets stuck.
Wins: Ripper costume

Zone 3 · Rail Yard

Night-blue concrete and a real train track.
The Crawler
Run along its back, dash through the steam, and cut its eye when it bites the ceiling.
Wins: Crawler costume

Can you escape?

Clear all 18 stages and Rivvy rides the last train out of the factory at sunrise. Then go back for all 54 stars.

Boss fights

Beat each boss in its own fight to win its costume and a new power for your runs.

Bosses

Giant machines that crash into your run. Each has a weak spot - learn it and cut it down.

The factory crew

Every robot in the factory has its own trick. Meet them all to fill your logbook.
