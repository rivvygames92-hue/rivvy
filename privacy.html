Privacy Policy

Rivvy: Robot Runner · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Rivvy: Robot Runner ("the game") uses. The short version: the game does not collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we do not sell or share any data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (best distance, coins, costumes, awards, daily tasks and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the game removes it. Settings > More > Reset Data clears your progress (it keeps your coins and sound settings).

In-app purchases

Coin packs are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details. We only learn that a purchase was completed so the coins can be added to your game.

Sharing pictures

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your result and opens your device's own share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with.

Game engine

The game is made with Unity. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information (such as device model, operating system version and crash reports) to keep its engine working. This is not linked to you. See Unity's privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if online features like cloud save are added), this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.
