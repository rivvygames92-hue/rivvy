Rivvy: Robot Runner

Found a bug, lost coins, or have an idea? Email rivvygames92@gmail.com and include your phone model. We read every message.

How to play

  • Tap to flip between the floor and the ceiling.
  • Swipe up or down to switch rails.
  • Swipe forward to dash-slice enemies ahead, swipe back to slice the ones chasing you.

Common questions

There's no sound. Check the Ring/Silent switch on the side of your iPhone, and the sound settings in the game.

I bought coins but didn't get them. Restart the game while connected to the internet. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I start over? Settings > More > Reset Data. This clears your progress but keeps your coins and sound settings.

Will my progress move to a new phone? Not yet - progress is saved on the device only.
